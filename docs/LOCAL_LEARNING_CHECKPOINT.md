## Direction and boundaries

LLMs stay external. They select independently verified explanations or worked
lessons inside BiC's chosen topic. The student attempts tasks and produces its
own decisions and response bytes; evaluation never calls the tutor. These
experiments establish neither open-ended distillation nor a benefit from LLM
packet selection over the procedural tutor.

Curriculum generation is deterministic and independently checked. Rehearsal
uses bounded seed descriptors; candidate optimizers remain separate. Evolution
may change declared training settings, while truth rules, evaluation boundaries,
and promotion criteria stay fixed. Tasks concern inert simulated objects and
exclude suffering, coercion, and real-world actions. The practical benefit
criteria are correct learning, retained abilities, probability error, and useful
compute expenditure, not capability growth at any cost.

The curricula have no fixed iteration ceiling, but their present domains and
the students' representations still limit what can be learned. The symbolic,
dialogue, and released visual checkpoints have not been jointly consolidated.
The next priority is graded language instruction and better learning across
earlier utterances, measured on fresh contextual combinations. The ungated
development probe also failed composition, so weakening promotion checks is
not a supported fix. Future experiments must use new held-out evidence after
changes, rather than tune against the audits reported here.
