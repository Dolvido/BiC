# Decision after the shared-rate comparison

Prepared before reading any execution scores from the live shared-rate study.
This memo uses the frozen [protocol](SHARED_RATE_TRANSITION_PROTOCOL.md) and the
completed [objective results](SHARED_OBJECTIVE_RESULTS.md). It changes no gate,
authorizes no extension or new experiment, and selects no home checkpoint.

## Admit the evidence before interpreting it

Require completed within-budget publication, authenticated full-state lineage,
both declared rate transitions, exact exposure/restart accounting and independent
raw-record verification. Make comparisons at the fixed final endpoint; report
all four endpoints without selecting a better intermediate checkpoint.
A failed or incomplete run stops interpretation and preserves its receipts;
it does not justify an automatic retry, replacement, or longer allowance.

Keep four questions separate: continued acquisition from the common 15,592
parent, the lower-minus-control rate effect, reusable definition-program transfer,
and retention. Fit is actual practiced material; development is reused held-out
text in a restricted grammar. Native action plus exact free-reply success on all
questions in both counterfactual episodes is the definition criterion. Easier
broad-bank anchor scores, prior-value answers and aggregate loss cannot replace it.

## Apply the unchanged criteria

The primary screen remains at least 5 pp mean gain across the 12 complementary
fit/development x binding/revision x family cells, no cell regression, and
strictly positive fit, development and each-family means. The separate secondary
screen remains at least 5 pp across six basis composition/sequence cells with no
cell regression. A mixed or failed effect is reported as such, even if retention
improves; a favorable slice cannot be substituted for either screen.

Absolute acquisition still requires at least 75% in every complementary cell;
basis capability requires at least 75% in every basis panel/family cell. Research
eligibility additionally requires positive six-cell transfer over original 9,760
and every one of the 480 retention checks per branch within the 5 pp loss limit.
Keep all five references: 15,592, 13,000, 10,408, 9,760 and historical 9,112.
Operationally valid completion and scientific eligibility are different claims.

## Select the next shared learning question conditionally

| Final result and full trajectory | Priority for subsequent review |
| --- | --- |
| Both remain below acquisition levels but practiced binding/revision and late native queries continue improving | Treat continued underfitting as live; evaluate whether another bounded whole-curriculum practice question would add information before changing architecture. There is no automatic extension. |
| Lower passes the primary effect screen | Retain the conditional rate benefit as evidence, then check absolute acquisition, transfer and all retention sets before considering it a research starting point. Passing a comparative effect alone is insufficient. |
| Lower fails the primary screen, or exchanges fit gains for development/retention losses | Do not declare a superior rate or start a rate sweep. Describe the tradeoff and select any next mechanism from the unresolved shared learning problem. |
| Practiced acquisition passes but development remains weak | Investigate generalization of the learned procedure across shared contexts and realizations; further memorization of the identical lessons is not the central test. |
| Complementary fit/development acquisition passes but basis composition/sequence remains weak | Investigate reuse of learned meanings in unfamiliar programs, with whole-program separation. Mastering practiced composed meanings is not yet reusable composition. |
| Both remain weak on actual fit, with little observed late progress in source/destination execution | Inspect shared language binding, intermediate state or temporal credit before another blind rate extension; choose one interpretable cross-family mechanism only after that review. |
| Acquisition/transfer gains coexist with retention failures | Keep both branches research-only. Consider one shared preservation/replay question rather than repairing individual subjects or replacing a failing historical reference. |

These are interpretation priorities, not extra acceptance thresholds or fixed
future experiments. Compare fit/development and binding/revision separately,
including every family and source/destination/prior-value query trajectory.
The final interval spans 1,296 updates, twice each earlier interval; raw interval
gains are not equally spaced slopes. The earlier objective control continued
improving on practiced revision while development binding fluctuated. A smaller
rate therefore tests stability, not an established excessive-rate diagnosis.

## Stop repetition when it no longer answers a question

Do not extend merely because losses fall, one family improves, a middle endpoint
looks better, or a numerical proof passes. If another pass or rate would repeat
the same unresolved ambiguity between exact-fit acquisition and program reuse,
stop that extension and first state what shared mechanism a new comparison would
distinguish. Do not alternate auxiliary removal and rate tuning without evidence
that the new comparison adds information. No cell-specific lessons or thresholds
follow from this memo. Retention failures remain visible even when fewer than
before; eligibility cannot be obtained by deleting references.

An observed rate benefit is conditional on the inherited AdamW state; effective
weight decay also changes with rate. Finite endpoints cannot establish an
asymptotic plateau, a home-hardware limit, or general English intelligence.
Learning per hour is descriptive only with complete preparation, training,
evaluation, restart and publication costs; loader speed alone proves no learning
benefit. This study introduces no teacher calls, so it cannot establish tutor
benefit, learned curriculum choice or progressive tutor independence.

The separately retained home learner remains 9,328. Even a scientifically eligible
research descendant needs an independent prospective fresh comparison against
the then-current home learner, covering unaided capability and retention, before
any adoption. Reused study banks alone cannot authorize that change.
