# Interpreting the foundation-order pilot

Written on 2026-09-17, after 02:37:45 UTC, **before reading any foundation pilot scores**. This is a prospective interpretation guide, not a change to frozen sources, data, budgets, scoring, or selection rules. It establishes no numerical promotion gate. The completed [capacity comparison](CAPACITY_STUDY_RESULTS.md) showed weak paired answers even on observed fitting examples, especially arithmetic; it did not isolate curriculum, representation, or optimization as the cause.

## What this comparison can establish

Both fresh learners use the development-selected width 192 and learning rate .001, one shared initialization, identical admitted lesson bundles, and 1,536 updates of three 32-episode microbatches. Only the first 1,152 updates' order differs: ascending depth with rehearsal versus a shuffled prefix. The last 384 updates use the same mixed suffix. Each arm receives 147,456 episode exposures. Sparse naming-only admission repairs preserve procedures, values, labels, and schedules; the original and admitted plans have separate identities.

A difference supports an order effect for this initialization, finite lesson set, and training recipe. It does not isolate the benefit of adding primitive practice, because both arms receive it. Earlier composition and capacity studies are historical context, not matched controls. One initialization is one initialization; checkpoints, domains, lengths, pairs, and the two schedules are not independent seed replications.

The curriculum shares direct facts, copying, and state updates across color, count, and switch. Depth is causal copy/update count, not episode length. Each depth occurs in 8-, 10-, and 12-turn contexts. Distractors are disjoint rather than adversarial interfering overwrites. Finite color cycles and switch parity can make different syntactic chains semantically equivalent. Withheld ancestry therefore tests a stated syntactic boundary, not a wholly new algorithm or open-ended English understanding.

## Keep four kinds of evidence separate

| Evidence | What it measures | Limit |
|---|---|---|
| Endpoint `train_fit` anchors | Answers on selected actual training realizations, separated by operator, depth, domain and length | Capped anchors do not measure the whole training distribution. There is no fitting curve. |
| Fixed `fresh` development cells at all eight checkpoints | Acquisition and later performance on admitted procedures with changed visible names and anonymous value realizations | Names and values change together; a gap cannot be assigned to either alone. |
| Endpoint fresh audit cells | A separate realization test after both learners finish | Exact transcripts are reserved; shared vocabulary and historical semantic equivalences remain. |
| `composed` cells | Answers on the specified held supervised ancestry partitions | Development covers depths 3–5; audit covers 2–5. Depth 2 has no separate development motif. Primitives are shared, not withheld structure. |

Fresh cells distinguish depth-zero direct facts, depth-one copy, and depth-one advance. Keep those three primitive groups separate before pooling. Report all domains and lengths, not only the largest gains. Each intended cell has 16 complete pairs: one additional correct pair changes its score by 6.25 percentage points. Show integer denominators and avoid interpreting a few pairs as a precise effect.

The primary evidence is final **paired action correctness and paired free-reply correctness**, together with known-answer accuracy, ASK recall, unsupported ASK on known questions, and earlier eligible pairs. Final questions are always known and opposite within a pair. High aggregate accuracy or ASK recall can coexist with failure to follow the changed causal fact. Preserve exact counts and distinguish equal-cell means from pooled question rates. Blank/reset controls test dependence on available input/history, not mastery.

For retention, show each fixed development cell from zero through all stage boundaries and the endpoint. The curriculum arm's declared post-stage reference for depth `d` is update `192 × (d + 1)`; follow that reference through 1,152 and 1,536 rather than retrospectively selecting its best checkpoint. If acquisition happens later, describe the observed trajectory instead of assuming it already existed. At early checkpoints the arms have different depth exposures by design; equal update count is not equal per-depth practice. At 1,152 both have consumed the same prefix multiset. Their common suffix is another shared intervention, not a no-training control.

## Interpret outcomes before choosing the next shared experiment

“Strong” and “weak” below describe the complete count-backed pattern; they are not new thresholds or a license to declare a winner. Any next experiment needs its own frozen hypothesis, development-only calibration, budget and held tests.

| Observed pattern | Supported interpretation | Small shared next comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Both arms weak on primitive fit **and** fresh primitive answers | This recipe has not demonstrated reliable acquisition even on selected observed examples. Poor composed transfer cannot yet diagnose a composition-specific defect. | Compare the existing byte sequence encoder with one learned utterance-summary encoder feeding the same causal learner. Use the same raw observations and all three domains, with no parser/state input; calibrate both equally. This tests one representation/credit-assignment hypothesis, not a capacity verdict. |
| Primitive fitting is strong, but fresh primitive answers remain weak | The learner fits some realizations without demonstrating stable reuse of their operations. The present fresh panels do not separate name from value sensitivity. | Compare fixed realizations with prospectively verified naming-only variants of the same procedures and values, preserving labels and total episode exposure. Keep independent name/value diagnostic panels and all domains; do not patch individual aliases or arithmetic cases. |
| Primitive fresh and retained answers are strong, but composed fit is weak | Primitive success is insufficient for longer dependency execution under the current training recipe. The difficulty may involve credit assignment, memory, or optimization. | Test a shared learned query-time state-refinement step versus the current one-pass learner, across every domain and depth. Supply only observation-derived representations; record the extra computation and equal calibration effort. |
| Primitive fresh answers and composed fitting are strong, but held composition is weak | There is evidence of fitting trained combinations without reliable transfer across the declared ancestry boundary. This does not establish failure of all compositional learning. | Test a broader shared dependency-mixture curriculum against the current mixture, matching operation/label exposure and keeping new ancestry partitions sealed. Vary structural diversity across all domains, not lessons chosen to repair particular failed cells. |
| Earlier primitive performance falls, then recovers during the common tail | Performance is sensitive to order and later rehearsal. Recovery is compatible with temporary interference or response bias; it does not prove preservation of a hidden skill throughout the decline. | Compare more evenly distributed rehearsal with clustered rehearsal using the same lesson multiset and total old/new practice. Measure acquisition and retention jointly, including the cost of earlier recovery. |
| Earlier primitive performance falls and does not recover in the common tail | The prescribed rehearsal/tail did not restore the measured old behavior within budget. This alone does not identify catastrophic weight loss or prove longer rehearsal cannot help. | Test one shared consolidation mechanism against matched joint/interleaved practice, fixing both old and new exposure. Use verified old targets, preserve every domain's retention vector, and reject gains obtained only by sacrificing new acquisition. |
| Both arms show broad fitting, fresh transfer, held composition and retention | The finite learning foundation becomes promising; schedule differences may still be small or realization-specific. | Replicate the declared comparison with fresh initializations and independently frozen examples, then test transfer to another shared dependency pattern. Do not promote from this single pilot or relabel its many cells as multiple seeds. |

These are conditional candidate experiments, not instructions to launch every branch. Select one broad bottleneck from the complete result, record competing explanations, and change one shared mechanism. A schedule advantage confined to one domain/operator is descriptive evidence, not grounds for a specialist patch or automatic allocator preference.

## Actions and generated replies can disagree

If paired actions improve while free replies do not, report both. A correct decision head does not establish a usable English response. If replies improve while actions remain weak, generated wording does not validate the official decision path. Compare query-level agreement, parseable replies, known/unknown denominators, and final paired answers before attributing disagreement to the decoder. Parseability alone is not correctness; disagreement can reflect different learned decision boundaries, not just spelling.

A broad mismatch across domains would justify a separate shared action/reply-consistency objective, using canonical training targets and independent teacher-free evaluation. Do not make action labels a shortcut input to the decision path, score teacher-supplied reply prefixes, or silently replace official actions with parsed replies. Consistency can make two wrong outputs agree, so correctness and held transfer must remain separate outcomes.

## Efficiency and the continuing loop

Report accuracy/retention vectors against both optimizer updates and measured local cost. Include 3,072 formal retained updates and 294,912 episode exposures, then separately account for execution probes, failed preparation, admission, verification, scoring, and any failed or discarded work. Materialization time is inside step time; packing is not fully isolated by that measurement. Worker wall includes nested work, and overlapping workers must not be added into elapsed time or called dedicated GPU hours. Endpoint performance per second alone can hide regressions and different exposure mixes at intermediate checkpoints.

A future immutable cache of authenticated packed lessons could reduce repeated generation and validation. Charge its construction once, preserve per-learner cursors and optimizers, and require exact inputs, gradients, updates and restart behavior before reporting a speed benefit. No such benefit is established here.

The continuing English loop should receive a verified development/retention vector, complete source/data identities and honest cost records—not audit scores or a single aggregate reward. Audit outcomes may motivate the next human-reviewed experiment; they must not become repeated lesson-selection feedback. The current pilot is prescribed training, not autonomous curriculum discovery. Curriculum truth remains independently checked, lessons remain compact and reproducible, and the intended benefit is accurate reusable learning without needless regressions or wasted compute. These simulated state tasks establish no claim about subjective experience or welfare.
