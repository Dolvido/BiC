failed to acquire its new composed distribution: complete practiced binding
pairs were 0/60 and practiced revision pairs 7/60 after 180 composed teaching
batches. The experiment asks whether sustained practice on that same verified
distribution produces acquisition and transfer while retaining earlier skills.

## Measurements and decision rules

The principal question is acquisition, measured by complete counterfactual pairs
requiring correct native action and generated reply on every query. Report the
full trajectory for each family in complementary binding/revision on both exact
fit and development panels. Acquisition requires at least 75% in all 12 such
family-panel cells. This is a prospective research screen, not a statistical
confidence claim. Also report destination-only and known-query accuracy so
correct unchanged-state or prior-value queries cannot conceal failed updates.

Keep the original capability screen: at least 75% complete pairs in every one of
the 12 basis family-panel cells. Report its components even when acquisition
fails; the screen must not hide learning progress.

Composition benefit is the mean final curriculum-minus-control difference across
the six basis composition/sequence family cells. It must be at least five
percentage points with no regression in any of those six cells. These are
structural-program holdouts, with functional equivalences in some domains.

Retention checks allow at most five percentage points of regression in each of
96 native checks (75 broad joint/known/unknown checks, nine older-definition
complete-pair checks and 12 basis complete-pair checks), against each of:

1. The candidate's own immediate update-10,408 parent.
2. The original shared update-9,760 research parent.
3. The fixed retained update-9,112 learner.

Candidate eligibility requires all complementary acquisition checks, all basis
capability checks, all three retention screens, and positive composition/sequence
progress against the original shared parent. The paired curriculum-benefit
screen is reported separately as the comparative claim. A failing branch remains
a research artifact. No checkpoint is automatically adopted even if these screens pass.
The offline home campaign subsequently advanced to update 9,328. The fixed 9,112
reference above is the historical reference of this experiment, not the current
home checkpoint. Replacing the home learner would require a separate explicit
comparison with its then-current state; this study does not authorize that change.

The independently maintained reader authenticates all raw score and step files,
recounts native metrics, verifies the repeated schedule and exact workload, and
checks branch-specific baselines and full-state restart evidence. Expected raw
coverage is 128 bank endpoints, 208 score files and 35,232 episode evaluations.

These are repeatedly inspected development banks, not a fresh blind audit. No
result here establishes human-level English, general intelligence, self-awareness
or unconstrained self-evolution. The experiment tests a general learning question
across all existing rule families without selecting a weak cell for repair.
