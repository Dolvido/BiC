# Shared program mechanism audit

This source-only assessment uses completed studies, ending with the [objective comparison](SHARED_OBJECTIVE_RESULTS.md). It does not inspect the active rate comparison. The evidence supports an unresolved multirule acquisition problem, not an architecture ceiling: control's practiced revision improved from 17 to 39/60 complete pairs while binding improved only from 8 to 11/60; held-out basis composition finished at 1/96 and sequence at 0/96. Low practiced binding prevents attributing the entire gap to unfamiliar text.

## Hypotheses constrained by existing evidence

The strongest shared hypotheses are difficulty retaining and selecting multiple word-to-program bindings, and insufficient acquisition pressure on their consequential applications. Parallel action/reply readouts can add disagreement, but they cannot explain every error: completed objective results also show wrong answers shared by both heads. Neither a fixed reply context nor entity-value supervision alone identifies the cause.

Current training already includes shuffled definitions, argument-role reversal, and [two- and three-clause complementary programs](../experiments/complementary_composition_curriculum.py#L224). Earlier definition training included CD. Therefore unseen semicolon syntax is not the current explanation. SC/CS/SCS/CSS remain structural holdouts; switch identities make some held-out programs functionally equivalent to practiced ones, so these banks do not establish unseen functions in every family.

The prior [tied rereading](RECURRENT_READ_RESULTS.md) and [entity retrieval](ENTITY_RETRIEVAL_RESULTS.md) interventions failed their bounded comparisons. They do not justify repeating generic extra reading or alias retrieval without a new distinction. The [earlier binding scaffold](ENGLISH_FRONTIER_RESULTS.md) also showed that a successful auxiliary readout need not improve native transfer. Conversely, [sustained composition](SUSTAINED_COMPOSITION_RESULTS.md) and [broad acquisition](SUSTAINED_ACQUISITION_RESULTS.md) improved with practice; a plateau has not been demonstrated. Removing state supervision [failed overall](SHARED_OBJECTIVE_RESULTS.md), so harmful state-gradient interference is not established.

## Conditional next question

1. **If the rate comparison improves both complete binding acquisition and retention**, continue a bounded, unchanged whole-curriculum control before adding mechanisms. Compare equal full passes from the same admitted parent, keeping all-family acquisition and retention criteria. Late revision growth alone is insufficient; the practiced multirule gap must also narrow.
2. **If both rates retain the practiced binding gap**, test one shared training-only rule-binding objective: a nonce-conditioned decoder predicts the currently defined ordered C/S/D sequence plus STOP from the causal encoder state. Compare encoder-connected supervision with an otherwise identical detached-encoder decoder control, retaining the existing native and entity-state objectives, examples and full incoming optimizer. Independent interpreters provide training labels only; no decoded program enters policy inference. This distinguishes useful representation pressure from merely learning an extra readout. Require improvements in complete native binding, held-out names/programs and retention; high rule-decoder accuracy alone would repeat the earlier scaffold mistake.

These are conditional proposals, not implemented experiments or adoption decisions. No model, archive, teacher, generation or runtime execution was performed for this audit.
