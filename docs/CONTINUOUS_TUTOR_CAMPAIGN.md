# Continuous local tutor and native learning campaign

The local tutor comparison operated once and failed its benefit and withdrawal
checks. The sustained acquisition experiment is testing whether more complete
curriculum passes improve the underlying learner. This new owner joins the
existing components into recurring, resumable research cycles. It is an
engineered controller; useful tutor benefit, learned self-direction, broader
English and indefinite compact storage still require evidence.

## One continuing research learner

Start only from the completed, committed sustained-acquisition endpoint, with
its original SharedStateContinuation identity, full weights and AdamW. Preserve
that original identity in every later checkpoint. A completed campaign can be
resumed with the same sources, teacher, evaluation banks and campaign seed.
Each invocation grants an explicit local wall-clock budget. There is no fixed
number of successful cycles and no score-based early stop.

A cycle has these durable stages:

1. Prepare a fixed six-chapter contract covering all three families, all six
   depths and all three lengths. Every family gets 32 episodes per update.
   Two teaching chapters are literal rehearsal; choices apply to every family.
2. Ask the installed local LLM once for a compact recipe, using only current and
   previous native development counts. Persist its request and result. The
   compiler supplies truth; the teacher cannot author labels or execution code.
3. Compile procedural, authored and common-withdrawal streams with the existing
   independently checked compiler. Both teaching streams have 108 updates;
   withdrawal has 108 updates and identical lessons for both branches.
4. A separate local GPU worker restores two branches from the same parent,
   trains each, evaluates native actions and freely generated replies, saves
   full state, restarts after teaching, and completes tutor-free withdrawal.
   No teacher request occurs in this worker. Both branches are evaluated at
   baseline, after teaching and after withdrawal on all five declared banks.
5. Apply the fixed native comparison, persist its full decision and continue
   from the selected research branch. This choice does not promote a release.

The unchanged architecture, rate 0.0003, auxiliary weight 0.3 and original recipe
are preserved. Campaign and cycle numbers determine admitted 63-bit phase seeds;
there is no per-family weakness allocation or retry-until-success search.

## Useful continuation and tutor independence

The authored branch is selected only when it gains at least five percentage
points on complete development pairs relative to the procedural branch; its
fit, retention and both transfer pair scores are no worse; no development
family loses paired success; and no family's known/unknown action/reply score
loses more than five points on any of the five banks. Its tutor-free withdrawal
must also preserve each family's known/unknown action/reply slices within five
points on development, fit and retention. Otherwise procedural practice supplies
the next research candidate. Before adoption, the selected candidate must
preserve retention pairs and every family's known/unknown action/reply scores
within five points of both the current parent and the fixed campaign-start
reference. Otherwise retain the existing parent while recording all spent
training work. Malformed, incomplete or unmatched comparisons raise an error
instead of selecting a branch.

Both complete branches and all discarded work remain in the record. Success
requires native outputs; teacher text, state-decoder outputs and good-looking
rationales are never substituted for learner behavior. The tutor still chooses
from verified chapter motifs and naming/value realizations. Arbitrary
explanations, ordinary conversation and new concepts are outside this grammar.
This controller measures behavior without the tutor and preserves successful
learning across cycles; it does not yet learn when or what to ask by itself.

The five banks are repeatedly observed development diagnostics, including the
structural/layout views generated from an audit partition. Using them for
continuation selection makes them selection data, not a pristine final audit.
Broader independent evaluation and new learning domains remain necessary.

## Time, persistence and actual work

The declared per-stage ceilings are 120 seconds for preparation, 60 for the
local author, 600 for compilation, 600 for worker setup plus execution, and 30
for selection. Reserve 60 seconds for final publication. Admit a stage only
when its complete declared allowance and the reserve fit. Propagate its
absolute deadline into authoring, compilation and the GPU worker; setup does
not reset the worker's clock. Clock admission is conservative and may leave a
pending stage when its allowance no longer fits.

Commit state after every completed stage. At a budget boundary, preserve the
pending stage and its immutable artifacts. Resume reuses any accepted teacher
choice or completed worker; it does not repeat them. A failed or unknown stage
has a terminal error receipt and no implicit retry. A child receives the same absolute stage deadline. A watchdog allows five
seconds for cleanup, then terminates only that owned child if it is still live;
termination and kill waits are each bounded at five seconds. Such interruption
marks the stage work unknown and forbids implicit retry. An observation timeout
is not a license to launch another worker.

An adopted learner advances 216 updates per complete cycle; retaining the
existing parent advances zero. Actual paired training always spends 432 updates and 41,472 episode exposures. Keep these
quantities separate. Include each author attempt/completion, CPU preparation,
all compiler work, archive loads, setup, GPU work, evaluation, restarts,
snapshots and publication. Inherited work is not charged as new work. Unknown
failed-stage work is retained in its local receipt and never reported as zero.

Protect evaluation transcripts and previous fresh teaching through compact
SHA256 inventories. Reuse the original admitted parent inventory without
regenerating its history. Only the first campaign census reads the old compiled
teaching streams. Subsequent cycles add their fresh transcript fingerprints. Original admitted
training transcripts remain allowed for literal rehearsal. They also have a
separate fresh-only exclusion: a newly compiled non-replay example that matches
one is rejected before a successful curriculum manifest is published.
Raw cycle archives and evidence still grow on disk; this implementation does
not claim indefinitely bounded storage. No old artifact is deleted silently.

## Validation and execution status

The pure policy and generic owner/data/worker boundaries receive focused tests.
Mocked orchestration establishes lifecycle behavior only. The first actual
local campaign must complete all stages, and its native records must be checked
before claiming a working recurring tutor campaign or learning benefit.
