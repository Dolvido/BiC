# Public release scope and remaining work

This public research snapshot includes the current paper-style README, source,
MIT license, a CPU inference demonstration of the lower-rate research candidate,
compact aggregate evidence, checksums and focused validation.

The [demo guide](PUBLIC_DEMO.md), [evidence guide](PUBLIC_EVIDENCE.md) and
[validation record](RELEASE_VALIDATION.md) define the tested public surface.
The unfinished requested-word diagnostic implementation is intentionally excluded;
its prospective protocol is retained. Historical documents describe the local
research archive and should not be read as fresh-clone instructions.

## What remains

- A portable fresh campaign initializer with locally provisioned tutor support.
- Full optimizer and curriculum history for exact continuation/reproduction.
- A measured before/after training demonstration, rather than inference alone.
- Independent replication on fresh data and another initialization or lineage.
- Native program composition, reliable tutor benefit and learned curriculum choice.
- End-to-end measurement of retained learning per total compute hour and long-run
  storage growth; compact archive prototypes still need home-loop integration.

The source release is a useful, inspectable research artifact. It does not establish
adult English comprehension or increasingly independent general intelligence.
The latest lower-rate experiment passed its acquisition-effect screen (+24.375
percentage points) and 479/480 retention checks, but neither branch met all
capability and retention requirements. No research candidate is adopted merely
because it ships as a demonstration.
