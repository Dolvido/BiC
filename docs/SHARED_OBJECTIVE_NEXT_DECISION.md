# Decision after the shared-objective comparison

This note is prepared before reading any scores from the live shared-objective
study. It interprets possible outcomes without changing its frozen protocol.
It authorizes no automatic extension, promotion or new fixed experiment.
Learning and retention take priority over optimizing execution machinery.

## Establish the result first

Require authenticated completion, exact incoming state, both objective
transitions, actual work, full-state restarts and independent raw-score recount.
Use the fixed final endpoint for decisions. Describe every fixed checkpoint;
do not choose a better earlier checkpoint after seeing the results.

Keep four conclusions separate: acquisition level and trajectory, the declared
zero-minus-control objective effect, definition-program transfer, and retention
against all four references. Fit and development are separate, equally weighted
views. Destination-query correctness and complete action-plus-free-reply pairs
matter; unchanged-state answers or action/reply agreement cannot replace them.

## Interpret the objective effect

A passing prospective effect screen supports a conditional acquisition benefit
from removing the auxiliary objective at this parent and exposure budget. It
does not by itself establish capability eligibility or harmful representation
gradients. Changed gradient norms, clipping and inherited AdamW dynamics can
mediate the effect. The same representation can encode rules and entity values.

A failed effect screen does not prove the auxiliary objective useful or harmless.
Report small, mixed and negative effects distinctly, including every family and
fit/development view. Do not respond by tuning individual cells or searching
post hoc for a favorable intermediate objective weight.

## Choose the next research question conditionally

| Observed result | Research priority to consider |
| --- | --- |
| Both arms remain underfit but continue improving broadly | Fixed further shared practice before architecture changes |
| Exact-fit acquisition becomes strong while development or definition-program transfer stays weak | Shared procedure/context variation with whole-program holdouts |
| Both remain weak with little late destination-query improvement | One broad computation or temporal-credit comparison |
| Acquisition and definition-program transfer improve broadly | Fresh evaluation with retention preserved before considering adoption |

The completed preceding study showed weak acquisition with late gains across
all families; it did not demonstrate a plateau. Falling aggregate loss alone is
insufficient evidence of program learning. Broad structural-transfer scores
measure a different task from definition-program composition and sequence.
Repeatedly observed banks and potentially equivalent programs remain limits on
any transfer claim. These priorities are alternatives for later review, not a
precommitted next experiment or permission to extend the current run.

## Preserve prior abilities and measure useful efficiency

Acquisition gains with failed retention remain research tradeoffs. Consider one
shared retention or replay comparison rather than family-specific repairs.
Research eligibility still requires every declared acquisition and capability
gate, positive original-parent transfer, and all four retention sets. Eligibility
is separate from the comparative objective-effect claim and never triggers
automatic adoption.

Compare equal exposure first. Gains per update or per elapsed hour may describe
the observed intervals, accounting for unequal endpoint spacing; they are not
new acceptance rules, convergence estimates or extrapolated future benefits.
Finite observed endpoints cannot prove an asymptotic plateau or a home-hardware
limit. Restricted models and grammars do not establish general English ability
or learned self-direction.

Any loader optimization must demonstrate an end-to-end benefit on the actual
learning loop before claiming more learning per hour. Faster isolated loading or
skipping auxiliary computation is not itself evidence of improved learning.
Include preparation, training, evaluation, restart and publication costs without
double-counting nested timings.

The retained home learner at update 9,328 remains on its separate lineage.
Historical 9,112 is a fixed study reference, not the current home state. Any
later adoption needs a prospective comparison with the then-current home learner
and fresh evaluation; this note makes no adoption or new-training decision.
