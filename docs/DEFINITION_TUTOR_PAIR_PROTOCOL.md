# Compare two shared chapter orders

This prospective experiment tests whether the external tutor's shared skill
progression improves reusable instruction learning. It continues the exact
update-9,760 research candidate from the completed basis probe, preserving its
full optimizer, rate, model, objective and runtime. That candidate failed
retention and is not the retained project learner. Update 9,112 remains the
fixed retained reference. No candidate is automatically adopted.

The local teacher has already proposed this permitted order: bind copy/source,
revise copy/source, bind source/destination, revise source/destination, bind
copy/destination, revise copy/destination. The procedural comparison teaches all
three binding chapters before all three matching revision chapters. A chapter
has eighteen updates, with equal color/count/switch allocation. Each binding
chapter precedes its matching revision in both orders.

Both arms use the exact same 108 admitted teaching images. These regroup the
unchanged original basis rows and prefix targets by shared practice category;
there are no newly sampled lessons. Pair membership, row/target associations and
within-chapter order remain fixed. Only the order of chapters differs. The
compiler and independent interpreters determine correctness. The tutor sees
aggregate development counts and the chapter menu, not held-out answers.

Each arm receives 108 teaching updates followed by 108 common updates without
tutor requests. Common practice comprises 36 basis, 18 prior-definition and
54 broad lessons. In each six-update group the order is basis, broad, prior
definition, broad, basis, broad. The basis portion cycles through all six skills
six times; broad practice covers its eighteen original depth/length cells three
times. All three families retain equal weight. This is withdrawal of the tutor's
ordering decisions, not withdrawal of useful rehearsal.

Measure the unchanged parent and steps 108 and 216 on all four basis, three
prior-definition and five original banks. Save and exactly restore full state
before each trained endpoint. Report both trajectories and all native action
and free-reply counts. The final endpoint is fixed; do not select an earlier
checkpoint because its scores look better.

The primary tutor contrast is complete all-query paired correctness on the
composition and sequence panels, averaged equally over their six family cells.
A demonstrated benefit requires at least five percentage points over the
procedural arm and no regression in any of those six cells. The separate
capability milestone still requires at least 75% complete pairs in every
family of binding, revision, composition and sequence. Binding and revision
improvements are reported as intermediate progress, not substituted for the
composition milestone.

Any proposed retained candidate must improve shared composition/sequence
performance over the current parent and meet all retention checks against both
the current candidate and the fixed update-9,112 reference: at most five points
lost on original bank/family joint pairs and known/unknown actions/replies,
prior-definition complete pairs, and basis complete pairs. Also check basis
retention from teaching to withdrawal. A rejected tutor contrast does not by
itself authorize the procedural candidate. This study records candidates and
evidence; adoption remains a separate explicit controller decision.

Use one inclusive 900-second worker allowance for both arms, with no deadline
reset between phases or branches, no automatic retry and no teacher calls in
the worker. Count actual updates, exposures, restores, snapshots and unknown
outcomes. If complete ordered input/target streams, full starting state, recipe,
objective and runtime are identical, one physical trajectory may serve both
labels. Report that as reused evidence with zero treatment contrast; never
invent a second physical update count or independent replicate. The currently
accepted recipe changes seventy-two teaching positions, so two trajectories
are expected.

One comparison measures this ordering choice under these conditions. It cannot
establish general LLM superiority, broad English mastery or learned curriculum
self-direction. The controller and prerequisites remain engineered. Increasing
native independence requires measured retained ability during later zero-request
learning cycles, not merely a running scheduler.
