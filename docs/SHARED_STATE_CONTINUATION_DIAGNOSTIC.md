The output directory is exclusive. A fixed 180-second allowance includes setup,
scoring, checks and publication, with deadline checks between operations; an
in-flight operation may finish later. Preserve partial work, error details,
wall/CPU cost and peak GPU memory on failure. Do not retry or extend silently.
The command requires the output directory, main directory, completed summary
and launch hashes, and diagnostic source/protocol hashes. The separate stdlib
verifier requires caller-pinned diagnostic, receipt and launch hashes and writes
an exclusive verification receipt; it performs no neural or archive decoding.

These are representation readouts on repeated training examples. Improved
auxiliary accuracy does not establish native use, transfer or comprehension;
poor readout does not establish absence of encoded facts. Keep conditional
value accuracy, binary knownness and full argmax distinct. The diagnostic
cannot override native-policy results, promote a learner or justify more
training on its own.
