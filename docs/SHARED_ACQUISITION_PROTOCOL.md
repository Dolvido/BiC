from a zero-pair starting point. The subsequent authored-curriculum comparison
completed the external tutor boundary but failed its benefit and withdrawal
checks. The next experiment therefore measures sustained acquisition, retention
and compositional transfer with the shared learner and full curriculum unchanged.
It does not allocate extra practice to weak families or request another tutor
recipe. This is a research continuation, not a release promotion.

## Decision and cost

This is one continuing learner, not a causal comparison of architectures or a
new tutor-benefit claim. Passing screens would motivate replication and broader
curriculum tests; it does not automatically promote a checkpoint or complete
the home-learning objective. Failing transfer after stronger acquisition changes
the next curriculum/representation decision; poor acquisition justifies further
diagnosis of common learning rather than repairs to individual test cells.
